Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Remember My Birthday (“the App”) helps you remember birthdays and prepare messages. This policy explains what data we use and why.

Data on your device

Birthdays, notes, drafts, phone numbers you choose to store, and reminder preferences are saved on your device (and in your iCloud account if you turn on iCloud sync). We do not sell this data.

Contacts, Calendar, Microphone, Speech

With your permission, the App can read Contacts and Calendar to import birthdays, use the microphone and on-device speech recognition to add a birthday by voice, and schedule local notifications. These features stay under your control in iOS Settings.

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, we store an opaque Apple user identifier and (when Apple provides it) your name and email on device so we can show your account and optionally send transactional email.

Email notifications

If you enter an email and enable email notifications, that address is sent to our companion server only to deliver welcome, sign-in, or birthday reminder messages via our email provider (SendGrid). You can turn email off or clear your address in Settings.

Companion tips

Basic tips run on your device. If you tap Enhance, a short prompt (name, relationship, a notes snippet, days until the birthday) may be processed on-device and/or by our companion server. We do not upload your full address book. Results may be cached on device.

Account deletion

In Settings → Account, choose “Delete account & data on this device” to remove birthdays and sign-in data from that iPhone.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub or use the support URL listed on the App Store listing.
